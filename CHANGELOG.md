# Changelog

## 0.11.0

- First publication of this maintained derivative, based on upstream 0.10.0.
- Retains the expanded 117-preset blood-gem catalog and registry-aware gem management.
- Adds English-first and Simplified Chinese language selection.
- Replaces borrowed raster graphics with reproducible geometric artwork.
- Omits narrative game descriptions, console saves and workstation configuration.
- Removes the upstream automatic updater; derivative updates are manual.
- Adds publication checks, Windows packaging and corresponding-source archives.

Validation boundaries and known limitations are documented in `docs/testing.md`.
